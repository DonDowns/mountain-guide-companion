Mount Lindsey access

An executed landowner waiver is required to enter Trinchera Blanca Ranch property for the designated Mount Lindsey summit access route.

Scope is hiking access to the summit on the designated route. The portal prohibits route deviation, hunting, camping, overnight use, motorized or wheeled transport, and drones on ranch property; it does not represent the route as maintained or supervised.

Before leaving service, confirm current Mount Lindsey access and waiver requirements; route conditions and group judgment govern.

Possession of this guide does not grant access.
